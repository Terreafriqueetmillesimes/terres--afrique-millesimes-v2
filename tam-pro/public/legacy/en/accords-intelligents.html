Methodology

The House signature

1. Reading the dish

We break each African dish down by its structure: the bitterness of n'dolè, the sweetness of plantain, the smokiness of the meat, the acidity of tomato, the depth of groundnut, the freshness of herbs.

2. Reading the wine

Tannin, acidity, residual sugar, bead, alcohol level, ageing. Every cuvée has its identity card — from the Médoc to Loire Chenin, from Champagne to Cognac, all the way to alcohol-free bissap.

3. The signed marriage

Marie Louise Ticki and her circle of sommeliers validate every pairing. No blind algorithm — human expertise, anchored in two continents and thirty years of practice.
